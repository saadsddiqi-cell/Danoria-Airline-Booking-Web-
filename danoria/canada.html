Nature

Wilderness Unmatched

Canada holds 10% of the world's forests and more lakes than every other country combined. From the Rockies to the Atlantic coast.

Banff National Park

Canada's first national park, established in 1885. Turquoise glacial lakes, snow-capped peaks, and abundant wildlife including grizzly bears, elk, and bighorn sheep. Lake Louise and Moraine Lake are iconic.

Niagara Falls

Three waterfalls straddling the US-Canada border with the most powerful flow in North America. The Canadian Horseshoe Falls drops 51m. Boat tours bring you within metres of the thundering cascade.

Northern Lights, Yukon

The aurora borealis dances across the Yukon and Northwest Territories from August to April. Whitehorse and Yellowknife are prime viewing spots with dedicated observatories and glass-roofed cabins.

Pacific Rim National Park

Vancouver Island's wild west coast — temperate rainforest meets crashing Pacific surf. The West Coast Trail is a 75km backpacking route through ancient cedar, sea caves, and First Nations heritage sites.

Architecture

Icons of the North

Canadian architecture bridges indigenous heritage with bold modernism, from the Gothic Parliament Hill to the futuristic CN Tower.

CN Tower, Toronto

553m communications and observation tower that defined Toronto's skyline since 1976. The Glass Floor and EdgeWalk — a hands-free walk on the outer rim — attract thrill-seekers worldwide.

Parliament Hill, Ottawa

Gothic Revival parliamentary precinct on the Ottawa River. The Centre Block's Peace Tower stands 92m tall. Free guided tours include the Senate, House of Commons, and Memorial Chamber.
