Nature

Forests, Castles & Alps

The romantic Rhine Valley, the mysterious Black Forest, the Bavarian Alps, and stunning Lake Constance — Germany's landscapes are postcard-perfect.

Neuschwanstein Castle

The fairy-tale castle that inspired Cinderella's castle and Sleeping Beauty's castle. Built by King Ludwig II from 1869, it perches dramatically on a Bavarian alpine cliff above the village of Hohenschwangau.

Rhine Valley

The Middle Rhine Gorge (UNESCO) is lined with medieval castles, vineyard terraces, and wine villages. River cruises between Koblenz and Bingen offer the most scenic German landscapes.

Bavarian Alps

The Zugspitze (2,962m) is Germany's highest peak. The Bavarian Alps offer world-class skiing, hiking, and the famous Berchtesgaden National Park with its turquoise Königssee lake.

Black Forest

Dense pine and fir forests covering 6,009 sq km in southwestern Germany. Home to cuckoo clocks, dark fairy tales (Grimm Brothers), and the source of the Danube river.

Architecture

From Gothic to Bauhaus

Neuschwanstein, Brandenburg Gate, Cologne Cathedral, and Bauhaus design schools that changed architecture forever.

Brandenburg Gate, Berlin

A 18th-century neoclassical monument and the most recognized symbol of Berlin. Once divided by the Berlin Wall, today it stands as the ultimate symbol of German reunification and peace.

Cologne Cathedral

A Gothic masterpiece that took 632 years to complete (1248-1880). Standing 157m tall, it was the world's tallest structure for 4 years. Its twin spires dominate the Rhine skyline.
