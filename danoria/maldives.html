Nature

Ocean Paradise

1,200 coral islands of crystal turquoise atolls, bioluminescent plankton beaches, pristine white sand, and sunsets that defy description.

Turquoise Atolls

From the air, Maldives atolls look like a scatter of jewels across the Indian Ocean. The 26 natural atolls comprise 1,192 individual coral islands, most uninhabited, all surrounded by sapphire lagoons.

Coral Reef Diving

Manta rays glide over Hanifaru Bay in numbers up to 200 at once. The Maldives' reefs host over 1,100 fish species, five species of sea turtle, whale sharks, and 200+ types of coral.

Bioluminescent Vaadhoo Beach

Vaadhoo Island's beach glows electric blue at night due to phytoplankton — a natural wonder that seems supernatural. Best seen on new-moon nights from July to February.

Whale Shark Snorkelling

South Ari Atoll has year-round whale shark sightings — the world's largest fish. Swimming alongside a 12-meter whale shark in open water is a life-changing encounter.

Architecture

Floating Elegance

Luxury overwater bungalows on stilts, glass-floor underwater restaurants, traditional Maldivian dhoni boats, and the ornate Islamic Centre.

Overwater Villas

Maldives invented the concept of the overwater bungalow in 1967 at Bandos Island. Today's overwater suites feature glass floors, private pools, direct lagoon access, and butler service.

Islamic Centre, Malé

The Grand Friday Mosque (1984) in Malé is the largest mosque in the Maldives. Its ornate Arabic calligraphy, wooden panels, and distinctive dome are the architectural crown of the capital island.
