Nature

Roof of the World

Mount Everest (8,849m), Annapurna Circuit, Phewa Lake, and the wild rhino grasslands of Chitwan National Park.

Everest Base Camp Trek

The classic Khumbu Valley trek to 5,364m takes 12-14 days from Lukla. Eight of the world's fourteen 8,000m peaks are visible from the base camp area. The Kala Patthar viewpoint is at 5,545m.

Annapurna Circuit

A 160-260km trek circumnavigating the Annapurna massif, crossing the Thorong La Pass (5,416m). The diversity of landscapes — subtropical valleys, high alpine deserts, Tibetan plateau — is unmatched.

Chitwan National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in the Terai lowlands, Chitwan is one of Asia's best wildlife reserves. One-horned rhinos, Bengal tigers, gharial crocodiles, and royal Bengal deer roam freely.

Phewa Lake, Pokhara

Nepal's most popular trekking hub has a stunning lake reflecting Fishtail (Machhapuchhre) and the Annapurna range. Sunrise rowboat trips, paragliding from Sarangkot, and peaceful Lakeside cafes.

Architecture

Sacred Valley Monuments

Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple), Boudhanath — world's largest stupa, and the medieval royal squares of Bhaktapur and Patan.

Boudhanath Stupa

One of the largest Buddhist stupas in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The colossal mandala of Boudhanath dominates the Kathmandu skyline. The circumambulation path (kora) is always busy with pilgrims.

Bhaktapur Durbar Square

A 12th to 18th-century medieval city featuring the 55-Window Palace, Golden Gate, and Nyatapola Temple. Bhaktapur has the best-preserved medieval cityscape in the Kathmandu Valley.
