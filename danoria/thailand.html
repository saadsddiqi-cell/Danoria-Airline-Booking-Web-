Nature

Tropical Paradise

Phi Phi Islands, Phang Nga Bay limestone karsts, Chiang Mai's northern jungles, and the Gulf of Thailand's pristine coral reefs.

Phi Phi Islands

Six islands of extraordinary beauty with emerald water coves, dramatic limestone cliffs, and vibrant coral reefs. Maya Bay (of The Beach fame) reopened in 2022 after a 4-year restoration.

Koh Samui

Thailand's third-largest island features palm-fringed beaches, the famous Big Buddha, secret waterfalls through the interior jungle, and world-class resorts along Chaweng and Lamai beaches.

Elephant Nature Park

Chiang Mai's ethical elephant sanctuary rescues elephants from the logging and tourism industries. Visitors can walk alongside, feed, and bathe with rescued elephants in a natural river setting.

Doi Inthanon National Park

Thailand's highest peak (2,565m) hosts two Royal Pagodas, misty cloud forests, and the most spectacular waterfalls in the north. Over 300 bird species make it a birdwatcher's paradise.

Architecture

Kingdom of Temples

The Grand Palace, Wat Phra Kaew (Emerald Buddha), Wat Arun's spire, and the ancient ruins of Sukhothai and Ayutthaya.

Wat Phra Kaew, Bangkok

Thailand's most sacred temple, located within the Grand Palace complex. It houses the Emerald Buddha, a 45cm jade statuette whose robes are changed three times yearly by the King himself.

Ayutthaya Historical Park

The ancient capital of Thailand (1351-1767), now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Hundreds of temples, Buddha statues, and the iconic tree-encased Buddha face draw thousands daily.
